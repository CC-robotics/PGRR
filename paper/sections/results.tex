\subsection{Imitation and Mask Ablations}
Table~\ref{tab:offline-ablation} compares Uniform BC, Margin-Weighted BC, and
DAgger on the same 399 scenario-disjoint validation states.  In this dataset,
margin weighting does not change the reported decision metrics and is therefore
not part of the selected method.  DAgger reduces expert-cost regret and improves
top-$k$ agreement.  Disabling the mask produces many invalid proposals, so the
no-mask rows are an offline diagnostic and were never executed on the robot.
These results support distribution aggregation and mask enforcement, but they do
not isolate the online safety supervisor from the learned policy.

\subsection{Interaction-Condition Analysis}
PGRR completes every crossing-flow and group-blocking condition and two of three
temporary-blockage conditions.  The remaining horizon-limited runs cluster in
head-on, doorway, blind-corner, opposite-stream, and overtaking interactions.
The generated failure taxonomy separates nine terminally stationary runs from
seven runs that retain short-window progress, including every blind-corner case.
This split points to two distinct extensions---deadlock resolution and
horizon-aware progress reasoning---without attributing either behavior to a
specific action unavailable in the aggregate result table.
